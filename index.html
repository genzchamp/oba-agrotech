COMMON QUESTIONS

What does OBA AgroTech help poultry farmers with?

OBA provides practical poultry-health guidance, preventive systems, digital decision tools, FHOF and farm-performance consultation.

How much is a Farm Health Consultation?

The current consultation is ₦5,000 or $5 for a 60-minute session.

What is FHOF?

The Farm Health Optimization Framework is a practical system built around observing risk, preventing avoidable problems, responding intelligently and continuously optimizing farm performance.

Are the digital tools free?

Selected tools are free to use. They are designed to give farmers useful value first, with paid products and direct support available when deeper help is needed.

Does a consultation replace a veterinary diagnosis or on-farm examination?

No. OBA tools and consultations are decision-support resources and should not be treated as a substitute for appropriate professional examination, diagnosis or emergency veterinary care.
