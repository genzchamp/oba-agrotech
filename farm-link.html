01 • FARM DECISION SUPPORT

Turn a farm question into a clearer next step.

Simple tools that work with information you enter. No invented live weather, prices or supplier data.

POST-HARVEST ESTIMATOR

Estimate the value of crop losses

Enter your harvest, estimated loss rate and selling price to understand the value at risk. This is an estimate, not a guaranteed saving.

Loss percentage and price are your own estimates. The tool does not provide market prices or promise that all estimated loss can be prevented.

FIELD-TO-STORE CHECKLIST

Prepare produce before storage or transport

Use this checklist to record key handling steps. Tick items as you complete them.

Storage requirements differ by crop. Do not apply one storage method to every produce type.

Add a community listing

03 • INPUT QUALITY & MARKET READINESS

Ask better questions before you buy or sell.

Simple verification prompts reduce avoidable mistakes. A listing or seller claim is not proof of quality or authenticity.

A / INPUTS

Before buying seeds, fertiliser or crop protection products

Check sealed packaging, manufacturer details, batch and expiry information, registration or approval where applicable, invoice and supplier traceability. Compare the product label with advice from a qualified local extension officer.

B / STORAGE

Before committing produce to storage

Ask about crop suitability, capacity, hygiene, moisture and pest controls, handling charges, insurance or liability, access dates and written terms. Inspect the facility where possible.

C / BUYERS

Before dispatching a harvest

Confirm buyer identity, grade and quality requirements, quantity, weighing method, price, deductions, transport responsibility, payment timing and dispute process in writing.
